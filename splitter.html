Bluesky Splitter

Paste text on the left and it's split into posts of up to 300 characters, breaking at sentences where it can and keeping posts roughly even. Add images to any post, then post the whole thread at once. Posting needs an app password, not your account password: create one in Bluesky under Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords. It's never sent anywhere except Bluesky, and is only saved in this browser if you tick "Remember password".
