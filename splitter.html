Bluesky Splitter

Paste text on the left and it's split into posts of up to 300 characters, breaking at sentences where it can and keeping posts roughly even. Add images to any post, then post the whole thread at once.

About app passwords

Use an app password, not your main password. Create one in Bluesky just for Splitter, so you can revoke it anytime. It's sent only to Bluesky, and saved in this browser only if you tick "Remember password". Splitter is meant to run as a local file, so it can't use Bluesky's OAuth sign-in.
